I don't build startups. I build small tools: the kind you find at 2am, use for
thirty seconds, and leave happy. It might be a text-to-speech page that works
without asking you to sign up, or an image uploader that gives you a link.

## Why small

With a small tool, the purpose is obvious. There's no onboarding, pricing page,
or "book a demo" message. The problem and the fix are both on the screen.

I can ship one in days instead of months. If nobody uses it, I've lost a week.
If everybody uses it, it can pay for itself.

## The rule I follow

> If it can't explain itself in one sentence, it's two products wearing a
> trench coat.

I learn what the project needs, build the smallest version that fixes the
problem, and then watch how people use it.
