I don't build startups. I build small tools — the kind of thing you find at
2am, use for thirty seconds, and leave happy. A text-to-speech page that works
without signup. An image uploader that just gives you a link.

## Why small

Small tools are honest. There's no onboarding, no pricing page, no "book a
demo." The problem is right there on the screen, and the solution is one
click away.

- They ship in days, not months
- If nobody uses them, the cost of being wrong was a week
- If everybody uses them, they quietly pay for themselves

## The rule I follow

> If it can't explain itself in one sentence, it's two products wearing a
> trench coat.

That's the whole philosophy. Learn what the project needs, build the smallest
version that solves the problem, then watch what people actually do with it.
